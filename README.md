# 💰 Payroll Bot

A Telegram bot that sends every employee their own salary pre-report to check and confirm before payday.

## The problem
Before salaries go out, each employee has to see their charges and bonuses and confirm them. I used to write to everyone one by one: thirty-plus employees, thirty-plus messages, calculations and questions. It wasn't hard, just heavy, and mistakes were easy to make.

## The solution
1. 📥 Charges, bonuses and absence deductions go into one payroll file
2. 🧮 The bot calculates each person's final amount
3. 📩 Every employee automatically gets their own salary pre-report: base salary, bonuses, charges and final amount
4. ✅ They check it and confirm, or ask a question **before** payday, not after

## Results
- Fewer mistakes in salary calculations
- Fewer "why is my salary less?" conversations
- Everyone sees exactly where each number comes from
- Hours of repetitive messaging every month, gone

## What I learned
The best automation isn't the fanciest one. It's the one that removes the task you dread every month. Building it with AI taught me that the key skill is explaining the process clearly, step by step, then checking and fixing the results.
